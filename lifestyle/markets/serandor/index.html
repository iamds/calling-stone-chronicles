The Enchanted Markets of Elyndra

Serandor Market

Where Winter Has a Mind of Its Own

Seelie Winter · The Seranstrae Dominion

Winter in Serandor is a spectacle of silver, white and a thousand shades of blue. Beneath the glittering towers of the capital, wooden stalls spread across a broad cobbled square, their awnings draped in shimmering fabrics and illuminated by lanterns filled with dancing frostlight.

The aromas of spiced pastries and steaming winter drinks mingle with the cheerful calls of merchants, while snowflakes drift between garlands of silver leaves and delicate blue icicles.

The Stall of the Whispering Snow Globes

Rows of exquisite glass globes stand upon polished silver shelves, each containing a miniature winter scene. Tiny castles, frozen lakes and snow-covered forests sparkle beneath swirling snow.

But pick one up, and the scene begins to change. A frozen lake becomes a moonlit skating party, or a tiny cottage reveals a welcoming fire glowing through its windows.

The globes are said to recognise the secret wishes of those who hold them, although they’re notoriously selective about their owners.

One particularly stubborn globe has refused to leave its shelf for seventy-three years.

Its elderly merchant insists it’s simply waiting for someone interesting.

A Market Full of Winter Wonders

Elsewhere, silversmiths display jewellery fashioned from enchanted frost, while embroidered snowflakes escape from scarves and flutter through the air.

Food merchants offer crystal-blue berries, silver-dusted pastries and tiny iced cakes that produce a flurry of snow when bitten.

One confectioner’s peppermint sweets leave customers breathing clouds of sparkling frost. He considers this excellent advertising.

As evening approaches, the lanterns brighten, musicians gather beneath the silver awnings, and the cobbles shimmer with reflected starlight.

Visitors may leave carrying exquisite winter treasures or a snow globe containing a world of its own.

But in Serandor Market, even the smallest winter wonder may have plans of its own.
