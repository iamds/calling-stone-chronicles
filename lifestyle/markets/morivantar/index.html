The Enchanted Markets of Elyndra

Morivantar Market

Where Beauty Has Teeth

Unseelie Summer · The Mirelands

Beneath a golden summer sky, Morivantar Market sprawls through terraces of living ivory, its stalls entwined with flowering vines and enormous leaves that unfurl to shelter passing customers.

The air hangs heavy with nectar, citrus blossom and overripe fruit. Brilliant flowers spill across marble fountains, while roots creep silently beneath the cobbles.

Everything is breathtakingly beautiful. Until something moves.

The Stall of the Hungry Blooms

Beneath an enormous flowering tree, a merchant displays the most exquisite plants in Elyndra.

Great scarlet blossoms open to reveal golden hearts. Sapphire-blue orchids trail silver tendrils, while delicate white flowers release perfumes capable of awakening the deepest longing.

But these plants do not merely attract customers. They desire them.

Some curl their tendrils around an admiring wrist. Others lean towards particular visitors, opening their petals to reveal tiny rows of glistening teeth.

The rarest flowers are said to bloom only for someone they wish to possess.

One enormous crimson blossom has remained closed for decades. Its merchant refuses to explain what happened to the last customer who approached it.

A Market of Beautiful Decay

Elsewhere, jewellers display emeralds entwined with living roots, while merchants offer silks that ripple like liquid sunlight.

At the food stalls, magnificent fruits burst with fragrant nectar, although some conceal blackened flesh beneath their brilliant skins. Honey-glazed pastries release clouds of golden pollen that linger around customers long after they’ve finished eating.

Beneath the market, roots twist through ancient stone, occasionally tightening around an unwary ankle.

As evening approaches, the flowers grow heavier, the perfumes sweeter, and the shadows beneath the stalls begin to stir. Visitors may leave carrying extraordinary treasures, beautiful plants or something they never intended to purchase.

But in Morivantar Market, the question is not always what you wish to take home.

It’s whether the market intends to let you leave.
