The Enchanted Markets of Elyndra

Veradessa Market

Where Everything is Growing, Including the Trouble!

Seelie Spring · Florindral

In Veradessa, the marketplace is never quite the same two days running.

Beneath flowering trees and cascades of wisteria, stalls fashioned from living branches twist and stretch towards the sunlight. Their canopies blossom in shades of pink, lavender and brilliant green, while butterflies drift between the merchants and their customers.

The air is filled with the scent of fresh flowers, sweet pastries and spring rain. Occasionally, a stall moves several feet overnight, leaving its unfortunate owner searching for it the following morning.

The Stall of the Impossible Seeds

Veradessa’s most celebrated merchant sells seeds unlike any others in Elyndra.

Displayed in tiny crystal jars, they glow, shimmer and occasionally rattle impatiently against their lids.

Each seed promises something extraordinary, although what it will grow into remains a mystery until planted.

One might produce a tree bearing silver fruit, another a flowering vine that sings at sunrise. Some grow miniature gardens that fit inside a teacup.

But the seeds are notoriously selective.

Those unwilling to be purchased simply vanish from their jars, reappearing when a more suitable customer approaches.

The merchant insists they know exactly what they’re doing.

He’s less certain that he does.

A Marketplace Bursting with Magic

Elsewhere, flower sellers offer bouquets that change colour with the weather, while jewellers display delicate necklaces woven from living blossoms.

At the food stalls, rainbow-coloured berries pop gently when bitten, and tiny cakes sprout edible flowers beneath their icing.

One baker has repeatedly complained about his pastries growing fresh leaves before he can sell them.

By afternoon, birds sing amongst the awnings, fountains scatter sparkling droplets over the cobbles, and the market hums with laughter.

Visitors may leave with beautiful flowers, unusual delicacies or a mysterious seed promising untold wonders.

But in Veradessa Market, there’s no telling what might be growing in your shopping basket by the time you reach home.
