The Enchanted Markets of Elyndra

Ebonspire Market

Where Nothing Is Quite What It Seems

Unseelie Spring · Ebonreach

In the shadowed streets of Ebonreach, Ebonspire Market flourishes beneath a canopy of twisted black branches and flowers that bloom without sunlight.

Deep violet blossoms spill from iron balconies, while luminous fungi illuminate narrow cobbled passages. The air carries the sweetness of night-blooming flowers, mingled with strange perfumes that seem almost familiar.

Merchants display exquisite treasures beneath dark silk awnings, although visitors are advised to look carefully before making a purchase.

Not everything here wishes to be recognised.

The Stall of the Stolen Reflections

At Ebonspire’s most notorious stall, hundreds of mirrors hang from twisted silver branches.

Some are no larger than a coin. Others stand taller than their owners, framed in black thorns and polished obsidian. But these mirrors do not always show the person standing before them. One might reveal a forgotten childhood moment. Another displays the face of someone long absent. The rarest reveal a memory the viewer never knew they possessed.

The mirrors are notoriously selective, refusing to reflect certain customers while eagerly revealing their secrets to others.

One ancient mirror has remained completely dark for centuries. Its merchant claims it is waiting for someone who has forgotten who they are.

A Market of Beautiful Deceptions

Elsewhere, jewellers offer rings that alter their appearance according to the wearer’s desires, while perfumers sell fragrances that awaken memories of places never visited.

At the food stalls, midnight-blue fruits reveal brilliant crimson centres, and delicate violet confections leave customers briefly hearing whispers from somewhere beyond the marketplace.

Some merchants specialise in flowers that bloom only when nobody is watching.

As darkness deepens, the market’s strange blossoms open wider, and shadows stretch across the cobbles.

Visitors may leave carrying beautiful treasures, unusual delicacies or a reflection they no longer recognise.

For in Ebonspire Market, the most dangerous thing you can lose may be the certainty of who you are.
