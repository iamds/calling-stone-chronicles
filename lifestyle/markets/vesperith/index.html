The Enchanted Markets of Elyndra

Vesperith Market

Where the Night Has Expensive Tastes

Unseelie Autumn · Messavros

Beneath a sky of perpetual dusk, Vesperith Market glitters with crimson lanterns, black velvet awnings and treasures displayed upon polished copper.

The air carries the scent of autumn roses, spiced wine and something faintly metallic. Yet beneath the splendour, flowers blacken at their edges, and magnificent fruits conceal rotting hearts.

Amongst the elegantly dressed Fae move other inhabitants of Messavros. Pale figures whose eyes linger upon exposed throats, and broad-shouldered strangers who pause to scent the evening air.

Nobody seems concerned. Perhaps they should be.

The Stall of the Velvet Masks

Behind curtains of deep burgundy hang hundreds of exquisite masks, fashioned from black lace, crimson feathers and delicate silver filigree.

Some grant their wearers another appearance. Others reveal hidden emotions or allow their owners to pass unnoticed through a crowded ballroom.

The rarest reveal the true nature of anyone who approaches their wearer. A valuable enchantment in a city where appearances can be dangerously misleading.

But the masks are notoriously selective, and their price is not always measured in crowns.

One magnificent black mask has refused every customer for more than a century.

Its merchant insists it is waiting for someone worthy of wearing it.

A Market of Dark Delights

Elsewhere, jewellers offer garnets that pulse like living hearts, while tailors display magnificent velvet cloaks that ripple without a breeze.

At the food stalls, black cherries gleam beneath crimson sugar, although some crumble to ash when touched. Deep-purple confections release fragrant smoke, leaving behind an appetite no amount of eating can satisfy.

Here, every bargain carries an obligation, and even the smallest gift may create a debt.

As midnight approaches, masked figures drift between the stalls. Somewhere beyond the crowded square, a wolf howls. A pale stranger smiles, revealing the briefest glimpse of sharpened teeth.

Nobody stops shopping.

After all, in Vesperith Market, danger rarely announces itself. It simply waits for an invitation.
