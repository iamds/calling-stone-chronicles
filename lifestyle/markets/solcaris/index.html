The Enchanted Markets of Elyndra

Solcaris Market

Where Sunshine Comes with a Little Mischief

Seelie Summer · Caelora

There are markets, and then there is Solcaris Market.

In the heart of Caelora’s magnificent capital, beneath the golden warmth of the Summer sun, colourful awnings, fluttering silks and bustling stalls await anyone with a few crowns to spend and a healthy sense of adventure. The market is at its best in the early morning, although arranging the merchandise can prove troublesome. Ribbons insist on tying themselves into elaborate bows, while enchanted slippers have an unfortunate habit of wandering away in pairs.

And somewhere amongst the laughter, bargaining and delicious aromas of freshly baked pastries stands the market’s most celebrated attraction.

The Stall of the Crooked Parasols

Hundreds of parasols hang from crooked wooden poles, their canopies fashioned from shimmering silks and delicate lace. Some offer shade from the fiercest sunlight. Others create their own tiny patch of sunshine, even beneath storm clouds.

One particularly beautiful parasol showers its owner with flower petals whenever they laugh. Another changes colour to match its owner’s clothing, although it has been known to express disapproval of unfortunate fashion choices.

The most expensive parasols choose their owners, refusing to open for anyone they consider unsuitable. The stallholder insists they have impeccable judgement. Not everyone agrees.

A Market with a Mind of Its Own

Elsewhere, golden sunberries glow when ripe, while the sweetest melons hum softly when tapped. At the jewellery stalls, delicate chains weave themselves around customers’ wrists, and tiny golden charms hold the warmth of a summer afternoon.

Bargaining is expected, although visitors should remember that the merchandise occasionally takes sides. A necklace might tighten indignantly around its display stand when offered an insulting price. By midday, musicians gather beneath the awnings, sunlight spills across the cobbles, and at least one merchant is chasing something valuable down a neighbouring street.

Visitors may leave with enchanted fabrics, unusual delicacies or a beautiful parasol. They may also discover that whatever they’ve purchased has a personality of its own.

After all, in Solcaris Market, shopping is never quite as straightforward as it seems.
