The Enchanted Markets of Elyndra

Veylstram Market

Where Shadows Have No Masters

Unseelie Winter · Frostleven

Beneath Veylstram’s starless skies, a market lies hidden amongst towers of black ice, where snow falls endlessly and pale blue flames illuminate stalls carved from obsidian.

There are no cheerful cries from merchants or musicians entertaining the crowds. Customers move silently between the stalls, their silver-black cloaks trailing across frost-covered stone.

Here, even the shadows seem reluctant to move.

The Stall of the Bound Shadows

Within a narrow chamber, hundreds of smoked-glass vessels stand upon shelves of black ice.

Inside each, a shadow twists slowly against the glass. Some are long and slender. Others resemble great wings, clawed hands or figures whose features cannot quite be distinguished.

The merchant offers shadows capable of concealing their owners from unwanted eyes, extinguishing light or silencing footsteps.

But these shadows are not easily commanded.

Some refuse to leave their vessels. Others cling to customers who have shown no interest in purchasing them.

And one, sealed within a vessel of ancient black crystal, has never moved at all.

The merchant will not discuss its price.

A Market of Frozen Wonders

Elsewhere, silversmiths display jewellery fashioned from midnight ice, while black mirrors swallow the reflections of those who approach.

At the food stalls, pale fruits lie beneath a coating of black frost, and delicate crystal sweets dissolve without flavour, leaving only an unnatural coldness upon the tongue.

Beyond the market, the endless snow continues to fall.

Visitors may leave carrying extraordinary treasures, but the silence of Veylstram has a way of following them.

And in Frostleven, not every shadow disappears when the light is gone.
