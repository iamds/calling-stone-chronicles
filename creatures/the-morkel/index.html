The Morkel

Predatory forest blood-feeder

A terrifying woodland predator, camouflaged against ancient trees, capable of detecting a living heartbeat through the roots and soil beneath its prey. It feeds on blood and can wait motionless for days before attacking.

Appearance: A large, powerful woodland predator with a body resembling twisted wood and dark, rough bark. Its long limbs end in hooked claws, while its head is crowned with irregular branch-like growths.

At rest, it can resemble part of a fallen tree.

Its particular ability: The Morkel can detect the pulse of a living creature through the forest floor. It senses movement through roots and soil, making it extraordinarily difficult to escape in woodland.

How it feeds: It attacks living prey, using sharp, hollow spines concealed beneath its jaw to draw blood. It does not necessarily kill immediately, and its victims may be found weak and bloodless.

Its most disturbing characteristic: It can remain motionless for days, waiting for something to pass close enough to attack.

Weakness: It cannot hunt effectively beyond living woodland. Open stone, bare rock and heavily salted ground interrupt its ability to track prey.

Place in Elyndra: The Morkel might inhabit ancient forests along the borders of Unseelie territories, although some could exist in remote, forbidden parts of the Seelie realms.

Unlike the Morkel it has no interest in politics, seduction or power. It is simply a terrifying predator.

How they differ from vampires

Creature What makes it dangerous
Vampire Blood sustains life; intelligence, supernatural abilities and social power
Sareth Blood allows it to steal another creature's physical identity
Morkel Blood is its food; it hunts through the living forest itself
