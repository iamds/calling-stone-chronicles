The Sareth

Blood-drinking shapeshifters

A dangerous, intelligent blood-drinking shapeshifter that must taste another creature's blood before assuming its appearance. It can reproduce their voice and physical characteristics, but not their memories. Perfect for deception, infiltration and political intrigue.

Appearance: In their natural form, the SARETH resemble tall, slender Fae, although their skin has an almost translucent quality. Their eyes are amber with a narrow crimson ring surrounding the pupil.

Their particular ability: They can assume the physical appearance of almost any living creature whose blood they have tasted. Unlike ordinary shapeshifters, they cannot simply choose a form. They must first acquire its blood.

This makes them extraordinarily dangerous infiltrators.

How they feed: Blood sustains their ability to transform. Without it, their borrowed forms begin to deteriorate and they eventually return to their natural appearance.

Their most disturbing characteristic: A SARETH can imitate someone's face, voice and movements, but cannot acquire their memories. A careful observer might notice that something isn't quite right.

Weakness: Running water disrupts their transformations, briefly exposing their natural form.

Place in Elyndra: Some Unseelie rulers might employ them as spies, while others would consider them too dangerous to admit to court. Even vampires might distrust a creature capable of assuming another person's identity.

How they differ from vampires

Creature What makes it dangerous
Vampire Blood sustains life; intelligence, supernatural abilities and social power
