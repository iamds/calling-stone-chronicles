Demons

Demons are powerful supernatural beings, often associated with Dark Magic, manipulation and the control of others. Although some may be summoned through magical rituals, others live freely within Elyndra. A Fae may possess demonic ancestry or be half-demon, making their true nature particularly difficult to recognise.

Like many of The Fae, demons can be extraordinarily beautiful. Their appearance may be entirely innocent, allowing them to move among the inhabitants of the Seasonal Courts without attracting suspicion. However, their eyes can sometimes betray their origins. Exceptionally deep colours, such as midnight blue, dark brown, almost black or rich ruby-red, may suggest the presence of demonic blood.

Many demons possess wings, although these are not always visible. They may also have retractable claws, concealed until needed. Their physical abilities vary according to their ancestry and the strength of their demonic nature.

One of their most dangerous abilities is their sensitivity to emotions. Demons can sense and experience the emotions of others, often recognising fear, anger, desire or uncertainty before an individual has expressed them. More powerful demons can influence these emotions, intensifying or suppressing them to suit their purposes.

Some possess the ability to manipulate another individual's mind, influencing thoughts, decisions and behaviour. A demon may encourage distrust, provoke anger or inspire affection, all without its victim recognising the source of these emotions.

Demons may be found within both Seelie and Unseelie Courts. Their presence does not necessarily mean they intend harm, although their abilities make them particularly dangerous when used for deception or personal gain. A demon may serve a ruler faithfully, live peacefully among other Fae, or use its powers to manipulate an entire court.

Their greatest danger may lie in the fact that the most powerful demons do not need to reveal what they are.
