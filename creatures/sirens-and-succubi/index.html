Creatures

Sirens and Succubi

Sirens and Succubi are among the most beautiful and dangerous female creatures found in Elyndra. Both possess extraordinary physical beauty, enchanting voices and the ability to attract and manipulate those who encounter them.

However, Sirens are creatures of the sea, while Succubi inhabit the land. Their beauty is almost impossible to resist, and their voices possess a magical quality capable of captivating even the most cautious individual. Although both species are predominantly interested in male companions, their intentions are not always immediately apparent.

Sirens

Sirens inhabit the seas surrounding Elyndra, particularly remote coastlines, rocky islands and dangerous stretches of open water. They are breathtakingly beautiful, with long, flowing hair, luminous skin and eyes that seem to reflect the changing colours of the sea.

Their greatest power lies in their singing. A Siren's voice can travel astonishing distances across the water, drawing sailors towards its source. Those who hear the song may become so entranced that they abandon their duties, steer their ships towards dangerous rocks or willingly enter the sea.

Some Sirens lure sailors to their deaths, leaving ships wrecked upon the rocks or their crews lost beneath the waves. Others select particular men to become their paramours, taking them into their hidden underwater dwellings or keeping them close to their coastal homes.

A Siren may become genuinely attached to a chosen companion, although her affection can be possessive and dangerous. A man who attempts to leave may discover that the beautiful creature who once enchanted him is unwilling to release him.

Sirens are particularly dangerous when singing together. Their combined voices can overwhelm the senses of entire crews, making escape almost impossible. Although they are sea-bound, Sirens can rise above the water and rest upon rocks or shorelines. Their power is strongest when surrounded by the sea, and they rarely venture far from it.

Succubi

Succubi are land-dwelling female creatures renowned for their extraordinary beauty, seductive nature and captivating voices. They may be found in forests, towns, palaces and the Seasonal Courts, often appearing indistinguishable from exceptionally beautiful Fae women.

Unlike Sirens, who rely primarily upon their songs, Succubi use a combination of physical attraction, voice and supernatural influence to entice their chosen companions.

Their voices are soft, melodious and deeply persuasive. A Succubus may encourage affection, desire or obsession, gradually drawing a man under her influence until he becomes unwilling or unable to leave her.

Some Succubi feed upon the life force of their victims, slowly draining their strength and vitality. A man may initially experience extraordinary pleasure and happiness, only to become increasingly exhausted, weak and dependent upon the creature responsible.

If a Succubus continues feeding, her victim may eventually die. However, not all Succubi wish to destroy their companions. Some select a man as a permanent paramour, maintaining the relationship for years and taking only enough energy to sustain themselves without killing him.

A Succubus may become fiercely possessive of her chosen partner, and jealousy can make her particularly dangerous. Those who attempt to interfere with such a relationship may find themselves facing a creature whose beauty conceals considerable supernatural power.

Succubi are especially suited to life within the Unseelie Courts, where their abilities may be tolerated or even admired. Nevertheless, their presence in a Seelie Court is not impossible, particularly if their true nature remains concealed.

Their Place in Elyndra

Both Sirens and Succubi are dangerous because their victims may willingly surrender themselves without recognising the enchantment influencing their decisions.

Neither species is necessarily murderous. Some form lasting attachments to their chosen companions, while others regard mortals and Fae as little more than sources of pleasure, nourishment or entertainment. Their beauty and enchanting voices make them difficult to recognise as predators until it is too late.
