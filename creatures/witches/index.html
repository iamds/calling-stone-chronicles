Witches

Witches are inherently magical beings who possess the ability to create and cast spells. Their powers may be used for healing, protection, enchantment or more dangerous purposes, including curses and Dark Magic.

Their abilities vary, with some witches possessing considerably greater magical strength and knowledge than others. They may work independently or gather in covens, sharing their knowledge and developing increasingly powerful spells.

Witches are found in both the Seelie and Unseelie Courts. Their presence is not necessarily regarded as dangerous, since magic itself can be used for good or ill. It is the way a witch chooses to use their powers that determines whether they are welcomed, respected or feared.
