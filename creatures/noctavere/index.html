Noctavere

A smoke- like shadow that creates illusions.

The Noctavere is a monster created by Smerdante. resembling nothing more than a dark, black mist or shadow, it is one of his most powerful creatures. It creates illusions so real, brother turns against brother and King against soldier. It creates and feeds from anger. Even Temerin, with his Seelie magic, cannot find the strength to fight it.

In Book 2 Gracie and the company encounter the Noctavere, in Ebonreach.

"From Book 2:

The cloud looms in the distance, a swirling, blackened mass that devours the light as it creeps closer. Its edges ripple, writhing like tendrils of smoke. Pulsing with a cold, malevolent energy it sends shivers crawling over my skin. Whispers rise—half-formed voices of despair that gnaw at the edges of my mind. It slithers toward us with silent hunger.

My heart pounds.Day plunges into darkness. Instantly cold. I pull my cloak around me, I am alone. I cannot see a single soul.

Shouts erupt from one side of the camp to the other.

“Get away! Get away!” One of the guards yells as he dashes through the grass. I back away from the confusion and stumble into Tavian. He stands, sword drawn, eyes wild. His hand grabs mine.

“I’ll keep you safe,” he says. “Did you see it?”

“See what?”

“The beast—with red eyes! Burning into the trees. I’ll kill it. I’ll save you. My Lady run! Do not let it find you.”

I stare up at him. A madness swarms in his eyes. A tendril of darkness curls its way around his arm, leading him away.

“Stop Tavian. It’s not real. It’s some spell. It’s making you seethings.”

Two of the guards fight each other while another two circle, waving swords and knives in their faces. All around me chaos ensues. Screams and shouts ring out in all directions. Somewhere, I hearTemerin’s voice chanting, but the verses sound thin – useless.

Whispers fill my mind. Smerdante’s coming for you. When he finds you, he’ll kill you. He’ll kill everyone. I push my back up against a tree. Amid the darkness I hear feet running, clangs of metal on metal and a plaintive meow.

Quinn. I stagger to my feet.

“Quinn? Quinn are you there? Are you alright?”

A plaintive whisper of a reply reaches through the shadows. Crawling forward on all fours, half-blind, I push into the dark. Quinn’s bright green eyes cut through the gloom, wide with desperation and panic. I pull him into my body. I stroke the top of his head, his back, the tip of his tail. He trembles.

“It’s OK Quinn. I’m here.”

He digs his claws into my shoulder. Sounds of fighting, anger and havoc fill the air.

“Why doesn’t Temerin stop it?” I whisper.

Quinn’s reply curls through the dark like a shadow itself.

“My Lady, he’s trying—but this evil is draining him.”

But more than that, as the two brothers fight, their anger at each other seething and writhing, Gracie is caught between them.

"Gripping Temerin’s staff, I run. The power of it hums in my hand.

The brothers circle like wolves, locked ina dance of death. Blades raised, breaths ragged. Hate soaring.

No. I won’t let this happen.

I throw myself between them, my arms stretched wide like a human shield. The staff trembles. I raise it high, the orb glowing brighter with each heartbeat.

“Stop! Dara! Tavian! Please—don’t do this!”

The shadows twist tighter, feeding on their fury.

My pulse thunders. I plant the staff into the ground. “Enough!”

A shockwave explodes from the orb—light, heat, power.

My power. It blazes down my arms, burning silver-blue.

Fae fire. My fire.

Too fast to stop, Tavian’s blade arcs toward me before he even knows I’m there.

I close my eyes.

Pain blazes white-hot across my upper arm.I scream.

“Stop! Just—stop!”

Blood soaks my fingers as I clutch my arm.

Symbolism

Danger, illusions, anger, death.
