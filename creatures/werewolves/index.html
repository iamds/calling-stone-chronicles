Creatures

Werewolves

Werewolves are powerful creatures capable of transforming between their human-like and wolf forms at will. They are not dependent upon the full moon to transform, although their strength and supernatural abilities are at their most potent during this time.

In their human-like form, werewolves are usually tall, exceptionally strong and powerfully built, with long hair and dark eyes. Their imposing appearance reflects the physical strength they possess, even when they are not in their wolf form.

They possess extraordinary strength, speed and endurance, together with highly developed senses of smell and hearing. Werewolves prefer to live and work in packs, forming strong bonds with others of their kind. It is rare to encounter a werewolf alone.

Those separated from their pack for extended periods can become increasingly unpredictable and dangerous. Known as rogues, these werewolves may lose the restraint and discipline normally maintained through pack life.

In Elyndra, werewolves are frequently employed as border guards, particularly within the Unseelie territories. They usually work in pairs, patrolling the boundaries and using their heightened senses to detect approaching strangers or potential threats.

Werewolves generally avoid close contact with other species because of the danger of transmitting their condition. A scratch, bite, tear or other wound inflicted by a werewolf upon a human, Fae or another susceptible creature will cause that individual to become a werewolf.

This transformation can occur regardless of the circumstances or the phase of the moon. Even the smallest wound can carry the curse. Although werewolves are most powerful beneath the full moon, their ability to transform and pass on their condition is never dependent upon it.
