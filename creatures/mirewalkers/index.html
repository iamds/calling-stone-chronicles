The MireWalkers

Living pieces of rot and mire shaped into brutish guards of Thornhelm, the Mire King. They are encountered at the border of the Mirelands and Ebonreach, near the place of the Three Kisses.

The Mirewalkers are men - like beasts formed from rot, earth and mire. Trolls from legend. They are the soldiers, servants of Thornhelm, King of Unseelie Summer, The Mire King, a High Fae King. They follow Thornhelm without question, fight for him, hold prisoners for him, obey his wishes without question. They are brutish, ugly, monsters. We first meet them early in Book 2, when Thornhelm meets Gracie for the first time. They have Prince Tavian as their prisoner.

From Book 2 'Strands of Shadow and Truth"

"The shapes don’t fade. Instead, they grow—massive lumps rising from the earth, dragging themselves up as if the land is surrendering itself to some hidden force. The creatures pull themselves out of the very earth, as if the ground is giving life to them. The land shifts and pulses beneath them, bending to their will as they emerge. Step by step they push closer. The sound of cracking twigs, sloshing mud and squelching grass grows louder. Their treads a low, rhythmic thunder fills the silence.

They take form. Their mottled, thick and grey-green skin blends with shadows and moss. Glimmers of wetness reflect off them, Their eyes, twin coals smouldering in the depths of their rough faces, catch the faintest light

I smell them—a damp, earthy scent, almost sour, mingling with the sweetness of the grass.

Their misshapen forms are wrapped in layers of grime and old moss, some even knotted with vines, like they’ve been growing here as long as the trees. And still, they come forward, relentless. I count five… no, six of them, each the size of a carriage, each moving with the weight of the earth itself.

"What are they,Quinn?" I say, barely above a whisper.

"They areMireWalkers. In legends and tales, they have many names. Trolls is the most common.”

Trolls. Not the kind in silly stories. Not the kind with big ears and funny songs. The kind that kill.

Our trek grinds to a halt. In the middle of the advancing creatures, Tavian is their prisoner, his arms wrapped with vines, holding him fast like roots reclaiming their own.
