Wraiths

Wraiths are mysterious, predominantly female supernatural beings associated with death and the passing of souls from one existence to another. They are neither entirely good nor entirely evil, although their appearance is rarely welcomed.

They are usually tall and slender, with long, flowing hair and pale, almost translucent skin. Their appearance is dominated by shades of white, silver and the palest grey. Their garments seem to float around them, moving as though caught in a breeze that nobody else can feel.

Although Wraiths possess no wings, they can fly, gliding silently through the air. They may also pass through walls, doors and other solid objects, allowing them to enter places inaccessible to ordinary creatures.

A Wraith will appear when death is approaching or has already occurred. Their purpose is to separate the departing soul from its physical body and guide it towards whatever existence awaits beyond death. In this respect, they resemble the Angels of Death described in mortal legends.

Their presence can be deeply unsettling. The air may grow cold, sounds become strangely muted and an unnatural stillness may settle over the surrounding area. Some Wraiths possess voices that resemble distant whispers, while others communicate without speaking at all.

Most Wraiths are solemn, detached and indifferent to the grief of those left behind. They rarely offer comfort, regarding death as a necessary part of existence rather than a tragedy. However, a few are capable of compassion, particularly towards frightened souls, children or those who have suffered greatly.

Wraiths are not generally considered responsible for causing death, although their arrival may be regarded as a warning that someone is about to die. They cannot ordinarily be prevented from carrying out their purpose, and attempting to interfere with a Wraith may have dangerous consequences.

In Elyndra, Wraiths are encountered in both Seelie and Unseelie territories. They answer to neither Court and recognise no mortal or Fae ruler. Their duty is to the departing soul, not to the living.

Although feared by many, Wraiths serve an essential purpose within Elyndra. Without them, souls might remain trapped between life and death, unable to complete their onward journey.
