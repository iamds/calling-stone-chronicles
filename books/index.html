The Books

Five novels tracing Gracie’s journey from discovery to courage, truth, belief, wisdom and dawn.

Book 1

Threads of Trial and Light

Discovery, courage and the spark of potential. Gracie's light and dyslexia become strengths as she begins to understand the fantasy realm and the power threaded through her. Gracie Fellows is sixteen, dyslexic, and fighting on every front. When her world cracks open she's torn into Limskada a brutal, beautiful realm of poisoned lakes, whispering forests and shadows with too many teeth. A silver haired mage and a sharp- tongued magical cat insist she's the prophecy girl.
At school, she fighting bullies who want her broken and classes her brain refuse to decode. At home her mother is too distracted to notice her new boyfriend Seth, watching Gracie like prey— his grip tightening every day.
And she's trapped on a kill or be killed quest in the Fae lands, with two princes who couldn't be more different - one of them already hating her guts, the other full of stories, laughter and friendship. Lethal sirens, cursed rivers, nightmare monsters: every trial designed to shatter her.
But the worst monster never left the real world.
Seth's closing in.
her best friend's in the crosshairs.
Two worlds, no safe places.
Some predators have claws.
Some just call themselves family.

Book 2

Strands of Shadow and Truth

Three kisses freely given
Three times the bargain made
Three kisses freely given
And bound in truth the trade
Gracie Fellows only wants to save a prince.
Instead, she makes a bargain that changes everything.
When a masked Hamlet steals three kisses at her school dance — and the moment is captured — Gracie finds herself taunted, watched, and followed.
In one world, a Fae king’s shadow stalks her. In the other, control closes in.
And at home, Seth’s menacing pressure tightens its grip.
Between magic, manipulation, and the stress of mock GCSEs, Gracie must fight to keep her voice, her choices, and herself.
Because some bargains never disappear.
They demand to be paid.

Book 3

Ribbons of Destiny and Belief

Gracie is taken.
In the Unseelie Court of Spring, the dark King Smerdante steals her memories. She no longer recalls her quest, the friends who fight beside her… or Tavian, the prince who would cross realms to save her.
In the real world, she is a prisoner of Seth — her mother’s ex-boyfriend — hidden in a crumbling house on a street marked for demolition. Drugged, isolated and running out of time, she must escape before his plan succeeds.
As war spreads across the fantasy realm and fear threatens to overwhelm her in the real world, fate tightens its grip.
Bound by destiny and belief, Gracie and Tavian share a fate neither crown nor court can deny.
But power always demands a price.
The King of the Unseelie Summer Court claims her as his own — bound by three kisses.
And in the real world… while GCSE exams wait like an axe ready to fall,
Seth is not finished with her yet.
Faith and identity are tangled with fate. Hope becomes a choice, and belief becomes something Gracie must fight to keep.

Book 4

Weaves of Wisdom and Resolve

Things should be easier for Gracie Fellows. Princess of Winter, Gleamweaver. Tavian by her side. But it all starts to unravel. Why is tavian so angry? Who is the spy in Winter? and why does it feel like everything is going wrong?
At Art college she's doing great. New friends, new projects– its all good – isn't it?
Seth's disappeared , but Tommy's training is all consuming, does he have time for her?
In both worlds things are falling apart.
Can Gracie pull the waves back together or will they split apart forever?
