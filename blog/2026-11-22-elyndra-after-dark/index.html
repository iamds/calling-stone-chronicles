Elyndra After Dark

A Traveller's Guide to the Entertainment Districts of Elyndra. Nine realms. Nine entertainment districts. Countless reasons to stay out after sunset.

Beyond the magnificent palaces, ancient forests and magical landscapes of Elyndra lies another world, one filled with music, laughter, excellent food, dancing and lively conversation.

Whether you prefer an intimate fireside supper, a bustling tavern, an evening of theatre or a night of rather less respectable entertainment, Elyndra has something to offer.

Every realm has its own distinctive entertainment district, reflecting the traditions, personalities and pleasures of its inhabitants.

So, where should a visitor begin?

The Seelie Realms

Elegance, enchantment and unforgettable evenings

The Seelie Courts are celebrated for their beauty, refined hospitality and spectacular surroundings. Yet beyond the grandeur of their royal residences, their cities offer lively entertainment for ordinary Fae and visitors alike.

Seranstrae Dominion — Seelie Winter

Capital: Serandor
Entertainment District: The Frostlight Quarter

Snow-covered streets, crystalline lanterns and welcoming doorways make Serandor's Frostlight Quarter a particularly enchanting destination after nightfall.

Inside its taverns, roaring fires provide a welcome contrast to the cold outside. Musicians entertain crowded rooms, while restaurants serve warming dishes and fine drinks. Elsewhere, dancing continues beneath ceilings glittering with magically suspended ice crystals.

The district is equally popular with couples seeking a quiet evening and friends looking for somewhere considerably livelier.

Where to go: The Frosted Glass

Serandor's best-known underground nightclub offers music, dancing, excellent drinks and plenty of opportunities to meet interesting company. Its private spaces provide somewhere quieter for conversation away from the main entertainment.

Best for: Dancing, lively evenings, romantic winter surroundings and escaping the formality of Court.

Caelora — Seelie Summer

Capital: Solcaris
Entertainment District: The Sunspire Terrace

Warm evenings, fragrant gardens and golden lanterns make Solcaris a wonderful destination for those who enjoy outdoor entertainment.

The Sunspire Terrace is filled with open-air restaurants, music, dancing and bustling gathering places. Diners linger beneath flowering canopies while performers entertain the crowds. Illuminated fountains and enchanted gardens provide a spectacular setting long after sunset.

There is an easy elegance to Summer's nightlife, where an evening meal can effortlessly become a celebration lasting until dawn.

Where to go: Solstice Haven

A popular establishment renowned for its excellent food, welcoming atmosphere and good company. Private dining spaces offer an opportunity for quieter gatherings away from the lively public areas.

Also worth visiting: The Inn at the End of the World

Situated in northern Caelora, this established inn offers an entirely different experience from the fashionable establishments of Solcaris.

Best for: Outdoor dining, dancing, warm evenings and romantic celebrations.

Amberfall — Seelie Autumn

Capital: Ambersorin
Entertainment District: The Embergard

Imagine streets illuminated by amber lanterns, richly coloured foliage overhead and the enticing aromas of food drifting from crowded restaurants.

The Embergard is renowned for its welcoming halls, gardens and spirited celebrations. Storytelling and poetry are particularly valued here, while musicians and performers provide entertainment throughout the evening.

Autumn's inhabitants enjoy good conversation as much as good food, making the district especially appealing to visitors who appreciate an evening that unfolds at a leisurely pace.

Where to go: The Amber King

A popular restaurant and club combining excellent food and drink with entertainment and private spaces for more intimate gatherings.

Best for: Fine dining, storytelling, music, poetry and convivial evenings.

Florindral — Seelie Spring

Capital: Veradessa
Entertainment District: The Blooming Crescent

In Florindral, entertainment flourishes among gardens, flowering trees and beautifully decorated courtyards.

The Blooming Crescent is a colourful collection of taverns, restaurants and intimate venues where music, art and dancing are part of everyday life.

Magically illuminated blossoms lend the streets a dreamlike atmosphere, while performers and musicians attract visitors from across the realm.

Spring's entertainment district is particularly suited to those who enjoy discovering smaller establishments rather than spending an entire evening in one grand venue.

Where to go: Flowerfields

A charming collection of intimate restaurants offering good food, drinks, dancing and private spaces. Ideal for a leisurely evening with friends or a romantic encounter.

Best for: Garden dining, music, art, romance and relaxed entertainment.

The Unseelie Realms

Mystery, indulgence and the freedom to enjoy yourself

Visitors unfamiliar with the Unseelie Courts might be surprised by the extraordinary variety of entertainment available within their realms.

The Unseelie enjoy fine food, music, dancing and companionship every bit as much as their Seelie neighbours, although their establishments are often less restrained by convention.

Some venues are welcoming and informal. Others cater to visitors seeking excitement, privacy or experiences rarely encountered elsewhere.

Frostleven — Unseelie Winter

Capital: Veylstram
Entertainment District: The Ice-Bound Vaultlands

Beneath Veylstram lies an extraordinary network of subterranean halls and passageways, transformed into one of Winter's most atmospheric entertainment districts.

Here, glittering ice formations surround intimate restaurants, lively taverns and music venues. Magical illumination dances across the frozen walls, creating a remarkable setting for evenings that continue long after the streets above have emptied.

Visitors can move between establishments without returning to the frozen world outside.

Where to go: The Buried Stalactite

An established favourite offering excellent food, drinks, music and dancing. Private rooms provide opportunities for more secluded gatherings.

Best for: Underground entertainment, atmospheric dining, music and memorable late nights.

Morevaine, the Mirelands — Unseelie Summer

Capital: Morivantar
Entertainment District: The Reedlight Docks

Forget polished marble halls and formal dining rooms. The Reedlight Docks offer something altogether more exuberant.

Built around Morivantar's waterways, the district comes alive after sunset. Lantern-lit taverns overlook the water, night markets attract noisy crowds, and gaming halls compete with musicians and performers for attention.

There is a wonderfully informal atmosphere here. Visitors are encouraged to enjoy themselves without worrying too much about appearances.

Where to go: The Rotting Willow

Despite its rather alarming name, this popular establishment is known for excellent food, drink, music and dancing, with private rooms available for those seeking quieter company.

Best for: Lively taverns, night markets, gaming, dancing and unrestrained fun.

Ebonreach — Unseelie Spring

Capital: Ebonreach
Entertainment District: The Shadow Quarter

Ebonreach's Shadow Quarter is a place of music, illusion, intriguing encounters and extraordinary entertainment.

Its shadowed streets conceal establishments offering everything from elegant dining to performances that challenge the senses.

Magical illusions are a particular attraction, and visitors may discover that appearances are not always what they seem.

The district is famous for its atmosphere of mystery and its willingness to accommodate a remarkable variety of tastes.

Where to go: The Shadowed Mirror

One of the realm's most notorious establishments, particularly popular with demons and vampires.

Its reputation suggests that few forms of entertainment are considered beyond consideration, making it an intriguing destination for adventurous visitors.

Best for: Magical illusions, unusual entertainment, mystery and decidedly unconventional evenings.

Messavros — Unseelie Autumn

Capital: Vesperith
Entertainment District: The Crimson Veil

Sophisticated, seductive and wonderfully atmospheric, the Crimson Veil is among Elyndra's most intriguing entertainment districts.

Its elegant taverns, exclusive restaurants and private establishments attract visitors seeking excellent food, fine drinks and stimulating company.

Music and dancing are important features of the district, although some of its most interesting entertainments take place behind closed doors.

Vampires and demons are particularly fond of its more adventurous establishments.

Where to go: Scarlett's Mask

An exclusive venue with a reputation for indulgence and entertainment without many of the restrictions found elsewhere.

Those seeking privacy, excitement or simply a memorable evening may find themselves reluctant to leave.

Best for: Exclusive dining, sophisticated nightlife, dancing, intriguing company and adventurous entertainment.

The Mortal Realm

Traditional hospitality and pleasures of a simpler kind

Limskada

Capital: Limska
Entertainment District: The Riverside Quarter

Not every memorable evening in Elyndra requires elaborate magic or magnificent surroundings.

Limska's Riverside Quarter offers a different kind of charm, one rooted in traditional hospitality, lively conversation and the simple pleasure of good company.

Timber-framed buildings line the cobbled streets, their windows glowing with candlelight. Taverns and eating houses welcome visitors, while musicians entertain customers in crowded rooms.

Market stalls offer food and locally crafted goods, and the riverside provides a pleasant setting for an evening stroll.

Although Limskada lacks the magical extravagance of the Fae Courts, its inhabitants know how to celebrate, and visitors may discover that an evening here is every bit as enjoyable as one spent in a royal capital.

Where to go: The Shaded Path

A well-loved eatery renowned for its good food, excellent atmosphere and welcoming hospitality.

Popular with locals and visitors alike, it offers a comfortable setting where mortals and Fae can meet on common ground.

Best for: Traditional taverns, hearty food, music, markets, friendly company and a more relaxed evening.

Discover Elyndra Beyond the Courts

From the glittering halls of Serandor to the lively waterways of Morivantar, from the mysterious establishments of Vesperith to the welcoming taverns of Limska, each realm offers its own distinctive pleasures.

Some visitors seek music and dancing. Others prefer fine dining, magical performances or the company of old friends.

Whatever your preference, there is one thing worth remembering:

Elyndra is not merely a world of kings, queens, ancient magic and dangerous secrets. It is a world filled with people who live, love, laugh and know how to enjoy themselves.

And sometimes, the most extraordinary adventures begin with an ordinary evening out.
