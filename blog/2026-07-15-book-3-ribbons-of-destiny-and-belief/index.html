Book 3 - Ribbons of Destiny and Belief

Book 3 of The Calling Stone Chronicles is now available from all good bookshops and Amazon.

Book Three - Ribbons of Destiny and Belief - is now in bookshops. I'm super excited to have three of the five books in the series out for people to read.
This one continues Gracie's challenges in both worlds. And this one has a masquerade as well!

But it also has her GCSE exams - all of them!

So this part of her journey is huge! And she learns a lot about her heritage, family.... You'll have to read it to find out more.
