The Elyndran Affinity

The Six Sacred Cycles of Elyndra – the ancient natural and magical order that governs the passage of time.

Elyndra is a world of Light and Shadow, where magic flows through the land, the seas and the skies. The passage of time is governed by an ancient natural and magical order known as The Elyndran Affinity.

The Affinity encompasses six sacred Cycles: the Sun, the Moon, the Stars, the Waters, Magic and Balance. Each follows its own rhythm, yet all are connected, forming the continuous passage of time throughout the world.

The Fae measure the longer passage of time through completed Affinities, while a season describes a passage of time without a fixed duration.

Although Elyndra's four Seasonal Courts, Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter, retain their distinctive climates, the Sun continues to rise and set, the Moon waxes and wanes, the constellations travel across the heavens, and the waters follow their ancient rhythms.

Each of the Six Cycles is honoured by a sacred celebration, observed throughout Elyndra.

The Six Sacred Cycles and Their Celebrations

1. The Cycle of the Moon

Midwinter's Moon: Acclamation of the Moon

An ancient celebration honouring the Moon and its influence upon Elyndra. The return of the Winter Moon marks an important occasion in the Affinity, recognising the Moon's connection with magic, destiny and the lives of The Fae.

2. The Cycle of the Sun

Midsummer's Day: Salutation of the Sun

A celebration honouring the Sun as a source of light, vitality and magical strength. Midsummer's Day holds particular significance for the Summer Court and is associated with the sacred Sunwoven ceremony, through which couples united by the mate-bond affirm their enduring commitment to one another.

3. The Cycle of the Stars

Affirmation of the Night

A celebration honouring the stars and constellations that illuminate Elyndra's night skies. The Fae acknowledge the connection between the heavens, destiny and the Moirai, whose influence reaches into the lives of those bound by fate.

4. The Cycle of the Waters

Celebration of the Bounty of Waters

A celebration recognising the seas, rivers and lakes of Elyndra. It honours the waters' power, the life they sustain and the extraordinary magical creatures dwelling within their depths.

5. The Cycle of Magic

Dedication of Elyndran Magic

A sacred observance devoted to the magical forces woven throughout Elyndra. The Fae acknowledge the ancient powers that sustain their realm and reaffirm their responsibility to preserve the magic upon which their world depends.

6. The Cycle of Balance

Affirmation of Balance and Truth

A solemn celebration recognising the delicate balance between Light and Shadow, Seelie and Unseelie, and the responsibilities that accompany magical power. It honours the belief that truth, justice and balance are essential to the continued harmony of Elyndra.

The Harmony of the Six Cycles

The Six Cycles are neither separate nor independent. Each influences the others, creating the enduring harmony known as The Elyndran Affinity.

Their sacred celebrations are recognised across the Seasonal Courts, although traditions and ceremonies may differ between the Seelie and Unseelie realms.

Together, they reflect the ancient relationship between the natural world, celestial forces and the magic that binds Elyndra.

Six Cycles. Six Sacred Celebrations. One Elyndran Affinity.
