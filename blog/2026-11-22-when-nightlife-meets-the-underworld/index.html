When Nightlife Meets the Underworld

Any establishment that attracts crowds, wealth and strangers may also attract less welcome attention.

Visitors familiar with Elyndra After Dark will already know that the nine realms possess a lively variety of restaurants, taverns, clubs and entertainment districts. These are places where friends gather, romances begin, musicians perform and travellers discover the pleasures of unfamiliar surroundings. Most exist for precisely those purposes.

But any establishment that attracts crowds, wealth and strangers may also attract less welcome attention. A pickpocket might favour a busy tavern. A smuggler could arrange a discreet meeting over supper. A dishonest merchant may use a private dining room to negotiate the sale of stolen goods. An influential criminal might even own a perfectly respectable establishment, providing excellent hospitality while conducting rather different business elsewhere.

None of this makes the entertainment districts inherently dangerous. It simply makes them part of the wider society in which they exist.

The same crowded room might contain a celebrated musician, a devoted couple, a dishonest trader, an off-duty guard and a noble whose private affairs would cause considerable embarrassment if revealed. And none may be particularly easy to identify.

Especially the noble.

Who Protects the Innocent?

Where crime exists, so must efforts to prevent it.

Across Elyndra, the protection of inhabitants and travellers may fall to guards, local authorities, rulers and others entrusted with maintaining order.

But the effectiveness and fairness of such protection cannot be assumed to be identical in every realm.

Some officials may be courageous and incorruptible. Others may be careless, prejudiced or susceptible to influence. A wealthy victim might find justice easier to obtain than a poor one. A stranger accused of wrongdoing could struggle to establish their innocence. And someone who possesses unusual abilities may be feared before their conduct has been properly examined.

There is also a fundamental difference between enforcing a law and ensuring that the law itself is just.

When authority is exercised fairly, it can protect the vulnerable.

When abused, it may become another instrument of oppression.

The question is not simply whether Elyndra possesses laws.

It is whether those entrusted with enforcing them deserve the power they hold.

The Most Dangerous Preconception

Perhaps the greatest mistake a traveller can make in Elyndra is believing that goodness and wickedness are easily recognised.

Light does not guarantee virtue.

Shadow does not establish guilt.

The word demon may conjure frightening images in the mortal imagination, but a demon's abilities do not determine their intentions. Neither do the distinctive characteristics of vampires, werewolves or any of Elyndra's other inhabitants.

A creature may be feared because it is unfamiliar.

A beautiful Fae may be trusted because their appearance is reassuring.

Both judgements can be disastrously wrong.

There are dangerous individuals in every realm, just as there are those who demonstrate courage, compassion and honour in the most unexpected places.

It is not what someone is, or where they come from, that reveals their character. It is what they choose to do.

And sometimes, discovering the truth requires the patience to withhold judgement until those choices become clear.

Beyond the Glittering Courts

Elyndra's underworld is not a separate kingdom of villains lurking beyond the borders of civilisation.

It is woven into the same society that produces magnificent architecture, extraordinary music, enduring friendships and remarkable acts of kindness.

Its criminals may be pirates, bandits, smugglers, assassins, dishonest merchants or powerful individuals who believe themselves beyond the reach of the law.

Its victims may come from any realm, species or station in life.

And its heroes are not always those wearing crowns or carrying official authority.

Sometimes they are ordinary inhabitants who refuse to look away.

Sometimes they are strangers who intervene when someone else is threatened.

And sometimes they are individuals whom society has already decided to call monsters.

So enjoy Elyndra's restaurants. Wander through its beautiful cities. Listen to its musicians, explore its markets and discover the extraordinary variety of life beyond the Courts.

There is much to celebrate.

But keep your eyes open.

In Elyndra, the most dangerous thing in the shadows may not be a monster at all.

And the greatest danger in the light may be someone everyone believes they can trust.
