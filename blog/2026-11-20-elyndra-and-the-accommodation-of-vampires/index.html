Elyndra and the Accommodation of Vampires

Elyndra welcomes most species – but some Seasonal Seelie Courts are less welcoming to those they perceive as agents of the Night, Shadow and Darkness.

Elyndra a world of Light and Shadow – welcomes most species. However, some Seasonal Seelie Courts are less welcoming to those they perceive as agents of the Night, Shadow and Darkness. Vampires and some other creatures belong to that category.

In Elyndra, the Vampire or Vampyre is seen as a creature that requires HUMAN blood to maintain its existence. It may try to survive on animal blood, but at some point the urge, need, compulsion to take some Human blood, Fae blood will surface.

Definition of a Vampyre/ Vampire – Elyndran Lore

The Vampyre or Vampire is a creature, male or female, who requires BLOOD to survive. It is usually MADE by an elder, senior vampire through a biting of the Neck or Wrist. It is necessary for a vampire, once bitten, to make a First Taste of a non-vampire, this is welcomed and encouraged and seals the Vampire bond. Vampires are always in bondage of a kind to their Maker, also known as an Elder or Creator. Vampires are immortal unless killed by the means noted below.

Appearance, Qualities and Attributes

As all Fae are remarkably beautiful in appearance, The Vampires are no exception. They are extraordinarily beautiful. They also have several things in common:

  • Very Pale, almost white skin.
  • Violet/ Purple/ or Deep Green eyes.
  • Jet black Hair.
  • Usually full, red lips.

The females – wear their hair long, at least to their waists. It usually hangs in a sheet of black silk-like strands.

The Males – keep their hair stylised to suit their personalities.

BOTH have a remarkable sense of style. Their appearance is immaculate. And After any feeding, the mouth is always wiped, usually with a lace edged handkerchief.

Qualities

All vampires have a highly developed sense of sexuality. It drives most of their behaviour and males and females alike are seen as potential to feed their sexual appetite.

They are rarely honourable. They twist the truth, although they like all Fae, cannot lie outright. Their words are dangerous.

They can compel with a stare and draw an individual into their will as soon as look at them.

Their ability to compel, persuade, lure an unsuspecting individual to make them do whatever they wish is often something a vampire will hone to a fine art. Because their ability to draw in an unsuspecting mortal or fae is so developed, it is recommended for an individual to wear a ring bearing the Solvarite crystal.

Vampires can move with astounding speed and cover vast distances very easily. Although they cannot fly and do not have wings their ability to leap and cover distance is a strong quality.

They also possess extraordinary strength. Able to bend metals, rip apart stone and tears things/ people/ animals apart with their fangs and when shown, their claws.

Their Fangs and Claws are usually hidden from view.
Eyes will turn to a feral darkness when they are about to bite.

Vampires found in Elyndra – cannot tolerate daylight for long. They can venture out and they can tolerate it for a while. However long exposure to daylight/ especially sunlight – will cause a sparkling glow to emit which makes their presence as vampire obvious to all. Prolonged exposure to sunlight / daylight will burn their skin.

Solvarite

Pronounced SOL-vah-rite

A rare, pale golden stone found deep within the Sunspire Mountains of Caelora, where centuries of exposure to Summer's elemental magic have infused the mineral with the power of sunlight.

Appearance: A translucent, honey-coloured crystal with fine golden threads running through its centre. In darkness, those threads emit a faint amber glow.

Its protection against vampires

  • When worn against the skin, Solvarite prevents a vampire from biting its wearer.
  • When a vampire approaches, the stone grows warm and its golden threads brighten.
  • Direct contact with the stone burns a vampire's skin, forcing it to retreat.
  • It cannot kill a vampire. Its purpose is protection rather than destruction.
  • However, if made into a blade – THE SOLVARITE BLADE WILL KILL IF IT PIERCES THE SKIN OF A VAMPIRE.

An important limitation: Solvarite only works when exposed to natural sunlight periodically. If kept in darkness for too long, its protective properties gradually weaken.

Destruction of Dangerous/ Deadly Vampires

Although it is hoped, visiting vampires will accept the rules of Elyndra, there are ways of killing vampires. It should be remembered that the drinking of Fae Blood, by a vampiric presence will give the vampire – immortality – it will walk the world forever. The Fae have naturally incredibly long lives, they live for centuries. It therefore follows that drinking of their blood will give some of their magical longevity to the vampire. IN the world of Elyndra this means the Vampire CANNOT be destroyed.

Death would need to happen in one of the following ways:

  • Severing the head from the body.
  • A Silver/ Wooden/ Metal/ Gold/Flint stake to the body.
  • Burning.
  • Lengthy exposure to Sunlight.
  • The Solvarite Blade – The Solvarite blade becomes lethal when it pierces a vampire's flesh, regardless of where it strikes?

(Flint is a hard, sedimentary form of quartz (silicon dioxide) that typically occurs as nodules or layers within chalk and limestone deposit it is one of the FIVE WEAPONS identified as deadly to the Fae, the others being – Salt/ Iron/ Rowan/ Flint/ StJohn’s Wort)

Location in Elyndra

As Elyndra is a Fae world and the majority of its inhabitants are Fae rather than human, the Vampyre is more than happy to drink, Fae blood as a means of surviving. Because of this, they are NOT welcome generally in the Seelie Courts of Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter.

The Seelie Courts, expect their Fae and especially their Royal Households to live, conduct themselves and behave with Fae/ Elyndran Law. Rules must be kept, within the tolerance of a particular realm. Advice given should be listened to, although it is usual for the King of each realm or Queen or both, to govern fairly and honourably. For these reasons and because Vampires rarely see the law as applying to them it is only if they are specifically INVITED by a High Fae ruler or member of a royal house, that they are, welcomed, accepted and allowed to roam freely. The same is NOT true of the Unseelie Courts.

The Unseelie Courts – all of which- have a tolerance for creatures frowned upon within the Seelie courts. Witches, Enchanters, Dark Wizards, Exiled Mages, etc. They do not judge or rule in the same way and allow for more natural predatory behaviour - where the Seelie Courts demand/ expect honourable and polite societal behaviour, the Unseelie Courts will allow less than perfect conduct.

Thus, vampires and other creatures find themselves drawn to the Unseelie Courts. Witches, Werewolves and wraiths are often seen and reside within Unseelie Courts.
