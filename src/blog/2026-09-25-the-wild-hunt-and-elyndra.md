The Wild Hunt come to Elyndra when there is an imbalance in the natural order of things. When light and dark magic is tipped, or when power is corrupted. 

They come to repair what is damaged. But this comes with a price. A bargain. And the price is always high, a life or more. 

The Wild Hunt is a reminder that  we need to respect the land, the skies, the wildlife in all its forms. We should be careful about walking into the unknown. We should remember there will be a payment required if we ignore the natural world and consider ourselves above it. The hunt recognises the changes of the seasons and how life and death works in a cycle. Often seen, when called, within the dark, cold months of winter, but it can be summoned any time. 

It should not be summoned lightly. There is always a price to pay, a deadly bargain to be made, a promise to be kept.
