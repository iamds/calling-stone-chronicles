Romantic partnerships form something important in Elyndra. As a Fae world it obviously links to magic. As we know, Elyndra wishes to always have balance between the Light and the Dark. 

A Mate-bond occurs on the expression of deep love between two individuals. Normally associated with The Fae, it is also possible for different species to find they are drawn to each other in this world. Fae and mortal, although once frowned upon is accepted. Sometimes such a union has to be fought for, sometimes Destiny intervenes. 

The Mate-bond is a mark. It appears like a tattoo. Depending on the status of individuals the Mate-bond can form, crowns, flowers, a special symbol or in the case of Gracie and Tavian is appears as a Golden Thread which sparked between them. This type of bond is rare, but when Destiny intervenes, anything can happen. 

Although the Mate-bond is the strongest and most powerful expression of a couple's feeling, it is further sealed through a ceremony known as Weaving.  Each seasonal Fae Court has its own version. 

Winter - Starwoven

Summer - Sunwoven

Autumn-Mist Woven

And Spring - Dawn woven. 

Such partnerships, once the Mate-bond has formed, rarely if ever become broken. Distance, sadness, grief and despair may weaken the sensation of the Mate-bond. But rarely, if ever, is it severed. There is no record within the lore of Elyndra of the severing of a Mate-bond. Both parties recognise, its beauty, strength and magical quality. To share oneself completely with another is recognised as the greatest gift two individuals can give each other.
