title: Marriages of Convenience - The political alliance.
date: 2026-09-05
summary: "In Elyndra, sometimes, a Fae court will make an alliance through marriage. "
---
Elyndra, being a Fae world, has its own special customs and ways of doing things. From Mate-bonds, where two individuals find each other and recognise they share a special, unique connection to each other, to The Weaving, a solemn ceremony usually following the two individuals who have already been mated. This is the occasion which seals the Mate-bond into something more. Something rare and precious, not to be severed. 

Fated-Mates, though not as common as Mate-bonds, occur in a time of chaos.

However, there are other alliances found in Elyndra. These are less about love and more about politics!

Because in Elyndra's history there have been wars, problems and the constant need to balance Light and Dark between the Seelie Courts and the Unseelie Courts, alliances between courts to strengthen partnerships and associations are not unheard of. 

These treaties or pacts are not a discussion or formal written agreement. They involve a marriage. A marriage of convenience. It can be separate from the Mate-bond, as the Fae recognise that cannot be challenged. But the Marriage of Convenience, or Political Alliance, can give strength to the weaker courts, solidify already connected courts and bring power. 

Sometimes that power brings good and positive results to all parties. 

Sometimes it causes trouble. 

Sometimes, the partners involved have other ideas, but the ruling of the King or Queen of a Fae court is law.
