Things should be easier for Gracie Fellows, Princess Gracielle, Gleamweaver, Princess of Winter, Tavian by her side.

But it all starts to unravel. Why is Tavian so angry? Who is the spy in Winter and why does it feel like everything is going wrong?

At Art college, she's doing great. New Friends, new projects— its all good— isn't it?

Seth's disappeared, but Tommy's training is all consuming. Does he still have time for her?

In both worlds things are falling apart.

Can Gracie pull the weaves back together? or will they split apart forever?
