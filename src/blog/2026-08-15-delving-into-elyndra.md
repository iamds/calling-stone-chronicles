Elyndra is the world in which The Calling Stone Chronicles takes place. As you can see from the website images, it contains several realms. Eight obvious ones belong to the Fae, one to mortals, who exist without Magic of any kind. The eight obvious realms are home to the Seasonal Fae Seelie and Unseelie Courts of Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter. Each realm is conditioned by its season. Seelie realms work with Light magic, Unseelie with Dark magic. 

But there is more to Elyndra. There are Hidden Kingdoms.
