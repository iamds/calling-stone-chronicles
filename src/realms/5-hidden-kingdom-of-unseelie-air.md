# The Hidden Kingdom of Unseelie Air

Beyond the frozen reaches of Unseelie Winter, where the last peaks vanish into cloud and the sky itself becomes a realm of endless storms ,lies the Hidden Kingdom of Unseelie Air. Few maps acknowledge its existence, for no fixed path leads there. Those who seek it soon discover that the winds alone decide who may pass beyond the veil.

If Seelie Air celebrates Freedom, Unseelie Air embodies the relentless force of Limitless Will.

Its vast citadels drift amongst storm-laden skies, suspended upon floating islands of dark stone and storm crystal. Towering spires rise into swirling cloud, their elegant forms linked by sweeping bridges that appear and disappear within the mist. Nothing feels permanent, yet nothing ever falls. The kingdom exists in perfect defiance of the natural laws that govern the world below.

Forged from polished black stone, dark silver and shimmering storm crystal, its architecture possesses both grace and immense power. Great halls stand open to the heavens, allowing the living winds to race endlessly through their chambers. Banners never cease their silent dance. Towers seem to move with the changing currents, while the sky itself is forever alive with drifting cloud and distant flashes of silver light.

The people of Unseelie Air are as untamed as the kingdom they inhabit. Fiercely independent and utterly self-reliant, they believe that anything which can be restrained will eventually weaken. To them, true strength lies in remaining unconquered—in thought, in spirit and in purpose. Their flowing garments mirror the endless movement of the wind, while their words are few, for silence often carries greater power than speech.

Travellers speak in hushed voices of the kingdom’s greatest wonder.The Sky Bridges possess neither pillar nor chain, yet they have never fallen since the dawn of Elyndra. They answer only to the ancient will of the kingdom itself. A bridge may carry one traveller safely across an endless abyss, while another finds only empty air where solid stone stood moments before.

Many who witness Unseelie Air describe it as beautiful beyond imagination.

Most also confess they have never felt so small.

For within this ancient kingdom, no king commands the wind.

No crown governs the sky.

And no soul, however powerful, can ever claim dominion over the storm.
