About the Author

Lesley McDowall writes YA fantasy full of dangerous beauty, emotional courage, mirrored worlds and characters who discover strength one choice at a time.

About The Calling Stone Chronicles

The Calling Stone Chronicles is a YA fantasy series built around mirrored worlds, emotional courage, manipulation, truth and the power of seeing differently. Across the mortal world and the fantasy realm, Gracie Fellows faces courts, creatures, bargains and choices that test who she is becoming.
