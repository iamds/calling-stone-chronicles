About the Author

Lesley McDowall writes YA fantasy full of dangerous beauty, emotional courage, mirrored worlds and characters who discover strength one choice at a time.

About The Calling Stone Chronicles

The Calling Stone Chronicles is a YA fantasy series built around mirrored worlds, emotional courage, manipulation, truth and the power of seeing differently. Across the mortal world and the fantasy realm, Gracie Fellows faces courts, creatures, bargains and choices that test who she is becoming.

What’s Next

Lesley’s latest writing, having completed “The Calling Stone Chronicles”, is her debut Romantasy novel ‘Within the Gilded Court’. Set within Elyndra’s Seasonal realm of Summer, it is an ‘Enemies to Lovers’ story with arguments, kidnaps, magic and attraction.

The Gilded Crown Novels · Book One

Within the Gilded Court

She’s thirty-nine, twice divorced, and quite capable of handling difficult men as well as difficult children! Unfortunately, she’s never encountered a Fae King.

Gaia McKensie knows her Shakespeare, loves her job teaching English and Drama, and has no intention of becoming entangled with another arrogant man.

Then she stumbles through a faerie ring into Elyndra, a world of dangerous magic, ancient rivalries and courts where beauty conceals deadly intentions.

And there she meets Caelis.

Caelis, King of Summer, is powerful, infuriatingly handsome and utterly accustomed to getting his own way. He considers Gaia an unwelcome complication. She considers him an insufferable tyrant.

Unfortunately, he has the power to bind her to his realm.

But Gaia isn’t a helpless heroine waiting to be rescued. Armed with a sharp tongue, a stubborn streak and a remarkable ability to quote Shakespeare at precisely the wrong moment, she refuses to surrender her independence, even to a king.

As their arguments become increasingly personal, an attraction neither wants to acknowledge begins to take hold. Yet beyond the splendour of the Summer Court lie dangerous creatures, treacherous bargains and powerful enemies who are watching their every move.

And when Gaia’s growing connection to Caelis threatens the very foundations of his court, love may prove more dangerous than any magic.

He rules the Summer Court. She refuses to be ruled.

An adult enemies-to-lovers romantasy filled with dangerous Fae, royal intrigue, sharp-witted banter, slow-burning passion and a heroine who knows that even kings can be fools.
